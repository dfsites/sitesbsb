Frequently asked questions

Which extension should I choose?

For businesses operating in Brazil, .com.br is usually the first choice. For an international audience, .com. In many cases it's worth registering both to protect the brand.

What if the name I want is already taken?

We suggest variations that keep the brand clear and easy to remember, and check for conflicts with well-known brands.

I already have a domain. Can you use mine?

Yes. We plan the switch of your current domain to the new website and email, preserving email records and redirects.
