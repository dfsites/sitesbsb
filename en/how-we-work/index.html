How we work

Everything agreed in writing, from assessment to operation

Digital projects go wrong when nobody knows what was agreed. That's why every stage, deliverable and commitment is made clear before we start.

Start my project See projects
Commitments

What you can expect from our team

  • Everything agreed before we start

    Scope, timeline and investment are defined in writing in the proposal. You know exactly what you'll get, when, and what it costs.

  • The domain is yours

    We register the domain in your name or your company's. It's your asset, not ours.

  • No lock-in

    If you ever decide to switch providers, we hand over the website files and guide the transfer.

  • Written communication

    Requests via form and email, with a history and an owner. We reply within 1 business day.

  • Checked before launch

    Before going live, we check the website on mobile and desktop, the forms, the speed and the structure for Google.

  • Analytics from day one

    The websites we deliver are ready to track visits, clicks and inquiries, so you can decide based on data.

  • Your team knows how to use it

    At delivery, we walk your team through what was built and how to request changes or update content.

  • No impossible promises

    We don't promise the top spot on Google or numbers we can't back up. We promise solid work and ongoing support.

Proposal

What's in the proposal

Before any development, you receive a written proposal with everything that will be done.

  • The project's goal and the problem it solves
  • Detailed scope: pages, features and integrations
  • What's included and what isn't
  • Timeline for each stage and the expected launch date
  • Investment and payment terms
  • Number of review rounds
  • Hosting, maintenance and support terms, if contracted
Stages

From assessment to operation

01 Assessment

We get to know the business, the audience and the goal. If there's already a website, we analyze what can be reused.

  • Initial conversation by email or online meeting
  • Analysis of the current website, if any
  • Recommendation of the best path

02 Planning

We define pages, content, technology and scope, and send a written proposal.

  • Sitemap of pages
  • Technology choice
  • Proposal with scope, timeline and investment

03 Development

Design and code built for your project, with agreed review rounds.

  • Staging address so you can follow progress
  • Review rounds defined in the proposal
  • Copy and images organized together with you

04 Launch

We go live with domain, security and analytics configured.

  • Domain, SSL and hosting
  • Redirects from the old website, if any
  • Final check on mobile and desktop

05 Operation

Hosting, maintenance and ongoing improvements, if you want to keep working with us.

  • Support from people who know the website
  • Updates and small changes
  • Growth: new pages, SEO and automation

Questions about working with us

How do I get started?

Fill in the contact form telling us what you need. We reply by email with initial guidance and next steps.

Do you work with clients outside Brasília?

Yes. We're based in Brasília, but we serve individuals and businesses across Brazil and abroad, with online meetings and written communication.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope and on when copy and images are delivered. The timeline for each stage is defined in the proposal before we start.

What if I don't like the result?

Development includes review rounds defined in the proposal, and you follow everything on a staging address before launch.

Who owns the website and the domain?

The domain is registered in your name or your company's. If you decide to switch providers, we hand over the website files and guide the transfer.

Can I start small?

Yes. No project is too big or too small: you can start with a single page and grow step by step.

Ready to get started?

Tell us what you need. Our team replies by email with the recommended path and next steps.
