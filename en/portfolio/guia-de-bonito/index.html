Guia de Bonito

Editorial portal about Bonito (Mato Grosso do Sul) in five languages, with site search and its own digital product. It became the engine for other travel guides.

Visit the website

Context

Bonito (Mato Grosso do Sul) welcomes tourists from Brazil and abroad, who plan their trip by researching tours, prices and itineraries.

Challenge

Publishing a large volume of content in several languages without losing performance and without maintaining five separate websites.

Goal

A fast, multilingual, well-organized portal for people planning their trip.

Solution

A static portal in Astro with MDX content, versions in five languages and site search. The foundation was designed as a reusable engine.

Architecture

  • Astro 7 with Tailwind and MDX
  • Site search with Pagefind
  • Internationalization structure
  • Deploy via script

Features

  • Editorial content on tours, itineraries and accommodation
  • Versions in five languages
  • Site search
  • Its own digital product (Guia Premium)

SEO

  • Multilingual structure
  • Sitemap and canonical
  • Internal link checks

Automation

  • Automatic link checks
  • Deploy via script

Results

  • About 640 URLs published (Sep 2026)
  • The same engine was reused for the Gramado and Foz do Iguaçu guides
