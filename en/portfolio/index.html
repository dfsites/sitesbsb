Case studies

Detailed case studies

Context, challenge, solution, architecture and results. Numbers only appear when they are documented in the project.

  • Calculadora DF Keeping hundreds of calculation pages consistent, fast and up to date, without relying on manual page-by-page review.
  • Avaliação de Imóveis DF The previous version was a single-page application that relied on JavaScript to display content, which makes it harder for Google to read. Contact requests had no organized place to be tracked.
  • Despachante Imóveis Brasília Organizing many services without creating repetitive pages, and keeping content consistent as the site grows.
  • Guia de Bonito Publishing a large volume of content in several languages without losing performance and without maintaining five separate websites.
  • Lagoa Santa Goiás Modernizing the portal without losing the history of URLs that were already getting traffic and without breaking old links.
  • Kit Contratos Imobiliários Explaining many contract types and several offers without confusing the buyer, while still being read well by Google, something typical React applications don't do well.
  • Vida com Deus Building consistency: following a long reading plan, day after day, without relying on spreadsheets, group chats or paper.
  • Igreja Presbiteriana Manancial Visitors and members didn't know where to find each piece of information; links were scattered across social media and messages.
  • Websites for gated communities and developments Each community has its own audience, properties and questions, and relying only on real estate portals means competing for attention with hundreds of listings. Building a website from scratch for each development would be slow and expensive.
  • Correspondente DF Credit is a sensitive topic: the website needs to build trust, be transparent and not promise approval.
  • Corretor DF Showing professional credibility (licenses, education, recognition) in an organized way, without turning into a tiring résumé.
  • Daniel Ferreira DF Provide a single address for business cards, social media and email signatures that leads each person to the right service.
  • 4D Desenvolvimento Pessoal Having a solid, easy-to-maintain and secure corporate website, without depending on platforms or dozens of libraries.
